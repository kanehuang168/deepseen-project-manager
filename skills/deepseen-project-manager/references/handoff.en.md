# Recovery and handoff

Record goal/completion, scope/exclusions, current inputs/versions, existing authority and budget, valid artifacts and real task identities, verification environment, open questions/owners/dependencies, revision ownership and rollback. Do not copy secrets, signed credentials or unrelated chat.

Existing authorization remains valid across turns when its scope, amount and validity still cover the action; recheck changes or expiry.

An accepted supplier task with a timed-out status read should resume via its original identity, not another charge. Old reports retain their true data time. Without a persistent scheduler, leave a resumable record instead of claiming background execution.
